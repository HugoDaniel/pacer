This score has one scalar track and two keys. At 120 bpm in 4/4, one beat is
0.5 s, so the segment runs from 0 s to 2 s and the midpoint at 1 s is 0.5.
The first key is reached structurally at t = 0, and the last at t = 2; the
runner asks for -0.5 s and 3 s as well, which hold 0.2 and 0.8. The thing
that usually surprises people is that there is no "start" or "end" keyword:
the boundary behaviour is implicit in the first and last key.
